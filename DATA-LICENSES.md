## Combined review outputs

The GeoJSON, CSV, statistics and embedded map combine or compare OSM-derived information and City-derived information. Treat the OSM-derived database component under ODbL and retain City attribution/terms for its component. No blanket MIT licence is granted for these datasets. Publication of this analytical package is not a determination that the City's licence may be imported into the OSM master database. That compatibility question remains unresolved in this audit; follow the OSMF process before an import: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/OGL_Canada_and_local_variants .
