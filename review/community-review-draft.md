# Windsor local OSM review: discussion brief

This brief accompanies an AI-assisted review package; it is not an approved import plan. The full scope, methods, evidence and limitations are in README.md.

Suggested starting points:

1. Confirm any existing permission or local process for Windsor municipal open data. The City licence, OSM Canada table and Contributors entry need reconciliation under current OSMF guidance.
2. Review the Aquatic Centre, Garage 2 and Capri charging sites as a small possible pilot. Verify exact equipment positions, station grouping, current operation and duplicate representations.
3. Review Richmond/Chilver and Banwell/Mulberry separately, with attention to road-arm topology, markings and amber warning flashers versus pedestrian traffic signals.
4. Use the remaining 713 screening records as a review queue, not as an automatic edit list.

No OSM edits have been made by this audit. Please advise whether an existing Windsor mapping initiative can take these candidates forward, and which sites would benefit most from a local survey. Any wider import or automated edit needs its applicable public review before execution.
